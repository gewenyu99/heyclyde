The pick

A. Carbon copy, because the personality is the product.

  1. It says what Clyde does. One agent writes, every agent gets a copy. The lift and its blue copy show that every time you choose something, without a mascot or a costume.
  2. It's clyde-v2, grown up. Same paper #f4f4f1, ink #20211f, blue #2f5fd0 and mark. What changed is everything around them: printed field labels, stamps, pens for agents, tear lines, the lift.
  3. It works everywhere Clyde shows up. System fonts pass the Worker's CSP and chat cards. Light and dark both pass 4.5:1, tested. It reads at 320px in a chat card and at 1,280px on the site.
  4. B is a costume and C is borrowed. The guild hall makes work look like a game and only works in the dark. The instrument looks like someone else's hardware and makes long memories hard to read.
What carries over from B and C

From the guild hall: quests have a number and get stamped when claimed. From the instrument: numbers are first-class, so confidence, recall counts and ages sit in mono, right-aligned, tabular.
