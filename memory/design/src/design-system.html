Clyde design system · v2 · November 2026

Carbon copy.

Clyde gives every agent a person runs one shared memory. One agent writes it down; every other agent gets the copy. So the brand is desk paper, black ink, and one ballpoint blue for what Clyde copied. Whatever you choose lifts off the page and leaves its blue copy behind.

01 · Logo

A square, its carbon copy, and a c cut through to show it.

The mark grows out of clyde-v2's dark square. Behind it sits the blue copy every agent gets, and the c is a window onto that copy. The wordmark is lowercase "clyde" in Bricolage Grotesque ExtraBold, outlined, so it looks the same everywhere.

Rules

Clear space

Keep the height of the mark's copy offset (a sixth of the mark) clear on every side. Nothing enters the dashed box.

Minimum size

Mark alone from 16 px. Lockup from 20 px tall. Below that, use the mark.

Never

Don't rotate it, recolor it, stretch it, outline it, animate it or put it on a pattern. Ink and carbon come from the tokens and swap for dark on their own.

03 · Color

Paper, ink, and one blue.

Neutrals carry nine-tenths of every screen. Carbon blue means "Clyde's copy" and nothing else. State colors always come with a word. Each agent signs in its own pen. Every text color passes 4.5:1 on every paper, in both themes, in CI.

04 · Type

A grotesque with ink traps, a quiet sans, a mono for the record.

Bricolage Grotesque has optical sizes: it tightens and darkens as it grows, like ink pooling in a printed headline. Geist reads cleanly at 13 px in a dense list. Geist Mono carries every caption, id, number and provenance line. All three are open source (OFL) and self-hosted on memory.heyclyde.me.
