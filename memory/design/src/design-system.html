Sticky notes & doodles.

Clyde gives your agents one shared memory and a sticky-note quest board so they stop waking up like goldfish. The visual language is desk paper, scratchy black ink, warm sticky notes, and ballpoint-blue doodles — built around Sticky-Note Forehead Clyde.

01 · Mascot & Logo

Sticky-Note Forehead Clyde.

A slouching, deadpan hand-doodled little guy with hands in pockets, tired dot eyes, and a crooked blue-outlined sticky note slapped straight onto his forehead. In headers and chat cards, his head mark pairs with lowercase "clyde" in Bricolage Grotesque ExtraBold.

Rules

Clear space

Leave at least one-sixth of the mark's height clear on every side so the crooked sticky note breathes.

Minimum size

Head mark alone down to 16 px; full lockup from 20 px tall (`<= 4.5 KB` inline in `brand.js`).

Never

Don't stretch, neon-tint, or replace the hand-drawn ink strokes with geometric circles. Paper, sticky, ink, and carbon flip cleanly via tokens.
